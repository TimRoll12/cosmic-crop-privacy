Privacy Policy: Cosmic Crop

Last updated: [DATE]

Cosmic Crop (the "app") is a mobile game published by Rolling Byte Studio ("we", "us").

Summary

The app does not collect, share or sell any personal information. It has no account, no advertising and no in-app purchases.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (crops, producers, Stardust, settings, and the date of your last visit) is stored only on your device, so that the game can keep growing crops while it is closed and give you your daily chest. This information never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app erases it.

Information we collect

None. We do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers or advertising identifiers. The app does not use analytics or crash-reporting services and does not connect to our servers.

Third parties

The app contains no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking tools.

Children

The app does not ask for any personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version changes how information is handled (for example, if we add ads or purchases), we will update this policy and the Google Play listing before releasing that version.
